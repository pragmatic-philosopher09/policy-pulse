This week in 60 seconds
AI-drafted · source-linked

One thing you can still do this week: the Standing Committee on Finance is taking views on the Indian Statistical Institute Bill until Sunday, 28 September — email comfinance@lss.sansad.in. Otherwise Parliament is in its post-session lull, and the movement is in the ministries. Health & medicine is the quiet surprise: drug rules amended twice, medical-device rules and the Pharmacy Commission Bill out for comment, and three committee reports on drug prices and affordability in August alone. Law, courts & policing is heating from a low base — the anti-paper-leak law, a Bill to add Supreme Court judges, the Bar Council's Advocates draft and now the Tribunals Reforms Bill. Money is busy but steady; the repo rate has sat at 5.25% for five meetings. Digital & AI recorded almost nothing formal, and the labour codes have moved into implementation, where PRS stops watching and the states start — Karnataka, Telangana, Jharkhand and Bihar all have platform-worker Bills.
