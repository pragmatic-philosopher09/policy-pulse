What the labels mean
Law passed
Parliament passed it (or it got the President's assent). It is now law, though it may take effect later.
Bill introduced
A Bill was tabled in Parliament. It can still change, be sent to a committee, or lapse.
Rules notified
The government issued binding rules, regulations or a notification under an existing law. No vote needed.
Cabinet approval
The Union Cabinet approved a proposal — usually the step before a Bill is introduced or a scheme launches.
Open for comment
A draft was published for public comment. Anyone can respond before the deadline.
Committee report
A parliamentary committee of MPs from all parties examined an issue and made recommendations. Not binding.
Scheme / programme
A government programme was launched, approved or expanded.
Court ruling
A court ruling or stay that changes how a law works in practice.
Update
A statement, report or development that doesn't fit the categories above.
